Optimal Pinning Synchronization of Hopﬁeld Chaotic Neural Networks
Based on DRL Algorithm and Application for Image Encryption

ARTICLE INFO                                   ABSTRACT
Keywords:                                      We develop a deep reinforcement learning (DRL)-based single-input pinning synchronization control
Image encryption                               framework for continuous-time Hopﬁeld chaotic neural networks and explore its application to image
Synchronization                                encryption. In the proposed framework, only one scalar control input is injected into a selected node
Deep reinforcement learning                    of the response system, and proximal policy optimization (PPO) is adopted as a representative DRL
Neural network                                 algorithm to learn the constrained nonlinear control policy. To support pinning-node selection, a multi-
                                               index local analysis is performed, and the drive–response synchronization process is formulated as
                                               a continuous-state and continuous-action reinforcement learning problem. The learned controller is
                                               evaluated under nominal and non-ideal settings. Based on the synchronized chaotic trajectories, an
                                               image encryption and decryption scheme is constructed using Logistic-map diﬀusion and dynamic
                                               DNA coding. Numerical results show that the local node-ranking result is generally consistent with
                                               the closed-loop synchronization behavior and provides practical guidance for selecting the controlled
                                               node. Compared with a ﬁxed-gain linear feedback baseline, the PPO-implemented DRL controller
                                               maintains eﬀective synchronization in several non-ideal scenarios and shows stronger adaptability
                                               under less favorable pinning channels. Finally, statistical analysis shows that the encrypted images
                                               exhibit reduced statistical redundancy, verifying the potential value of the proposed framework in
                                               chaos-based secure image encryption. These results suggest that DRL-based single-input pinning
                                               control is feasible for synchronizing Hopﬁeld chaotic neural networks under constrained actuation
                                               and limited observation.
